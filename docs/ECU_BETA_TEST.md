# Unassisted ECU student beta script

Status: prepared; no external student test has been completed. Fill in the actual verified HTTPS beta URL only after deployment. The tester should be an ECU student who has not seen Semester OS and who can use their own authorized timetable/material-plan documents. Obtain permission before recording their screen; keep all academic evidence and backups private.

Ask the student to speak their thoughts aloud. Record what they do; avoid explaining controls or interpreting results for them. Offer help only after they are completely blocked, and log that intervention.

1. Open the beta URL and explain what you think Semester OS does.
2. Choose ECU and select your academic context.
3. Upload your timetable. Optionally upload your material plan.
4. Explain which extracted values you trust and which are uncertain.
5. Correct at least one value, confirm the review, and generate your semester.
6. Find today's schedule and your next classroom.
7. Mark attendance, add an assignment, and inspect one course workspace.
8. Change the theme and find the motion/accessibility controls if desired.
9. Install the PWA, close it, reopen it, and confirm your semester/theme persisted.
10. Export a backup and explain where you would keep it safely.

Record each task as independent success, success after help, failure, or not attempted. Record time spent, confusion, friction, extraction errors/missing fields, unexpected results, and every point where developer explanation was needed. Separately record browser/device, native installation result, close/reopen result, and whether offline tools were explicitly prepared. Do not put names, student numbers, timetable images, original files, or exported backups in GitHub issues or public screenshots.
